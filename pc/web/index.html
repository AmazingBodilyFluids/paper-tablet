Tablet screen

While it's on, the tablet shows your strokes as you draw, plus the calibration targets. Your notebooks stay safe: the notebook app pauses and comes straight back when you turn this off or unplug.

Trail stays for

E-ink can't fade smoothly, so a stroke stays for this long after you lift the pen, then clears in one refresh. Slide all the way right for Until cleared: strokes stay until you press Clear the tablet screen below or a Clear ink button on the panel.

Shortcut panel
16 buttons you choose yourself. Tap with a finger or the pen. Customise the buttons
Finger gestures
Three fingers swipe left: undo, right: redo. Two fingers drag to pan, pinch to zoom.

Fingers are ignored while the pen is near the screen, so a resting hand does nothing.

Image strength
Move and zoom it on the tablet
While on, one finger drags the image and two fingers zoom it; gestures pause. A panel button can do this too ("Move image").
Trace mask
Shaded parts of the tablet screen ignore the pen, like a stencil, so you can only draw inside the shape you leave open.
Paint the mask on the tablet
While on, the pen paints the shading, the eraser end clears it, and one finger drags the whole mask. Windows gets no pen meanwhile.
Front light to full
Lights the screen evenly for tracing. It's a front light, so it lights the image on screen rather than shining through paper laid on top. Your light level comes back when the tablet screen turns off.

Shortcuts turn gestures into keys for your drawing app. Windows touch makes the tablet a touchscreen: apps scroll with inertia, pinch and rotate by themselves. The pen still mutes fingers while it's near.
